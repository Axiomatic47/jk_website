Independent legal researcher and pro se civil-rights litigant. Three federal actions filed and briefed since 2025: in the District of Columbia against federal officials and technology companies; in Minnesota and the Eighth Circuit over the Attorney General's statutory duty to investigate consumer fraud; and in the Southern District of Florida in equity.
